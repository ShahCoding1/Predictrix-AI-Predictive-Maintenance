Stratified train/validation/test splitting, preprocessing pipelines, fixed random seed and tracked comparison metrics.

Model dataset origin is shown above. Synthetic demonstrations must not be presented as real industrial validation.

Model predictions are research outputs, not a replacement for certified monitoring, physical inspection or safety controls.
